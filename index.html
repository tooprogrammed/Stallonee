Profile

The Story

STALLONEE is a Los Angeles–based Italian DJ and producer building a sound that sits between euphoric and unsettling — warped basslines, flickering textures, and grooves that don't let go. Raised on Italian club culture and sharpened on LA dance floors, his sets move like a slow-building storm.

Part of the extended 3PIECE family, with recent shared sets including a run at Catch One LA.
